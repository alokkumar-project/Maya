## About Maya V1

Maya V1 is a conversational AI chatbot built completely from scratch using **Python** and **NumPy**, without relying on deep learning frameworks such as TensorFlow or PyTorch.

The project implements the core components of a Transformer architecture, including tokenization, embeddings, multi-head attention, encoder-decoder blocks, feed-forward networks, and the training pipeline.
